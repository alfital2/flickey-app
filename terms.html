6. Privacy

FlicKey processes typing locally and does not upload your documents, messages, keystrokes, or browsing history as part of switching or conversion. Layout preferences, learned auto-fix exceptions, trial state, and activated license details are stored on your Mac.

The app uses Sparkle to check the update feed at flickey.site and download releases from GitHub. It contacts Lemon Squeezy to activate, periodically validate, and deactivate licenses: activation sends your key and Mac's name; validation and deactivation send your key and activation instance ID. These requests contain no typing content. Network errors during revalidation leave an activated license intact; an explicit invalid-license response removes it.

Purchase data (name, email, payment details) is handled by Lemon Squeezy at checkout under their privacy policy. Diagnostic reports and blocked-word feedback are shared only after you review them and choose to send them through your email or sharing app. See the Privacy Policy for local storage, request data, and the separate website and checkout traffic.
